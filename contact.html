Before you write

Questions we get most

Do you deliver outside Varanasi?

Yes. We ship across India by courier, and free above twenty-five jars. Cold weather routes can delay a parcel by a day or two, so we mention that before you pay.

Can I taste before I buy?

Please do. Three jars are always open at the counter, and we will tell you which one suits your palate instead of pushing the expensive one.

Do you supply shops on credit?

For local shops, yes, after three months of regular cash orders. For outstation stockists we work on advance payment only.

How long does a custom batch take?

Ten working days in ordinary season and eighteen during Diwali week. Label artwork needs two days before that, so send it early.
