Ghar Ka Tarika

How the jar is made

Four patient steps, the same way it has been done in our kitchen since 1948. Nothing is hurried, and nothing is short-cut.

01

Seasonal sourcing

Raw mangoes from Ratnagiri and UP orchards, picked green and firm at the right maturity.

02

Stone-ground masala

Ghar-resham chilli, turmeric, fennel and mustard crushed fresh on stone, never powdered.

03

Sun-dried & brined

Three days in the sun with salt and mustard oil, until the mango loses its sharpness.

04

Slow fermentation

Resting in brass jars for weeks until the masala settles and the pickle finds its depth.
