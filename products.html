What goes into every jar

Printed on the back of each label, so you never have to guess.

Mustard oil

Wood-pressed, poured hot to seal the jar and lock in the masala.

Ghar-resham

Single-crop chilli from Guntur. The colour, heat and body of the pickle.

Rock salt

The only preservative we use. Brine-strengthened, never iodised.

Whole spices

Fennel, cumin, mustard seed, nigella and cardamom, cracked not powdered.
