1948 The Origin Batch

A stall, not a shop

Kamleshwar ji started with a humble wooden tray outside Varanasi’s Gandhi Market selling freshly stone-ground spices. When a neighbour asked if he could preserve surplus summer mangoes, the first trial brass jar was sealed with hand-pressed mustard oil.

1972 The Spice Milestone

The masala stone

Ghar-resham single-crop chilli from Guntur entered our family formula. We began grinding spices exclusively on our deep black granite stone (sil-batta) so the friction produces warmth rather than scorch—allowing essential oils to bloom deep into the oil.

Ghar-resham chilli sourcing Hand stone ground masalas
1998 Expansion & Craft

Glass, and a wider map

We moved into our permanent storefront with a brass tasting counter. We replaced old bulk containers with thick, sterilized glass jars to protect the live fermentation, and started shipping parcel-packed crates across India to families yearning for authentic home taste.

Our rules

Six things we will never put in a jar

A short list, kept by everyone who works here. If a customer asks what is in our pickle, this is the whole answer.

1

No preservatives

Salt, mustard oil and time. Nothing that extends life artificially.

2

No artificial colour

If a pickle is bright red, it came from ghar-resham chilli. Not a dye.

3

No vinegar

Acidity comes from hing and fermentation. No sourness added.

4

No ready-made masala

Our blend is ground in-house every batch, adjusted by hand.

5

No factory kilns

Sun-dried in the courtyard, never force-dried in a hot room.

6

No rush

A jar leaves the fermenting room only when it tastes ready.
