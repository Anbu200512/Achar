Questions

Before you order

What is the minimum order for wholesale?

Twenty-five jars. Below that we would rather you buy retail, because hand-packing smaller quantities costs more than the jars are worth.

How far ahead should I book festive hampers?

Ten working days for Diwali, six for weddings. Peak season runs September to December, so talk to us in August if you can.

Can you make a milder version for children?

Yes. We can drop the chilli level to one or two, increase the jaggery, or leave out garlic entirely. Tell us the age group and we will suggest.

Do you ship outside India?

We ship to eleven countries. Food import rules differ, so we declare conservatively and send the batch card with the parcel.

How long does a jar actually keep?

Nine months for achar, a full year for murabba and honey preserves. Keep the oil level above the masala and use a dry spoon every time.
